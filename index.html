    About this project

    This is an educational overview for young readers, so it simplifies. Life was very different for rich and poor women, in cities and villages, and for women of different religions and ethnic groups in the same country. The rights scores describe what the law allowed for most women in that year.

    Marriage ages are rounded. Recent figures come from national statistics offices (UK Office for National Statistics, US Census Bureau, Rosstat, TurkStat and China's census); earlier figures are estimates from historical studies of census and church records.
